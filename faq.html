check-in and check-out

what time is check-in and check-out?

check-in begins at 3:00 pm and check-out is by 11:00 am. early check-in and late check-out may be arranged in advance, subject to availability.

can i request an early check-in or late check-out?

yes, subject to room availability. please contact us ahead of your stay to make a request.

booking and cancellation

what is your cancellation policy?

cancellations made at least 48 hours before check-in are fully refundable. cancellations made after this window are non-refundable.

do you require a deposit to confirm a booking?

a valid credit card is required to guarantee your reservation. full payment is collected at check-in.

general questions

is parking available on-site?

yes, complimentary parking is available for all registered guests.

are pets allowed?

we welcome small pets in designated pet-friendly rooms. please inform us in advance so we can prepare accordingly.

rooms and amenities

what amenities are included in the room rate?

all rooms include complimentary wifi, air conditioning, a smart tv, and access to the fitness center and pool. breakfast is included with select room packages.

can i request a specific room or floor?

room and floor preferences can be noted at booking and will be accommodated where possible, though they are not guaranteed.

family and group stays

do you offer connecting or adjoining rooms?

yes, connecting rooms are available on request, subject to availability. please let us know when booking.

is there an extra charge for additional guests or children?

children under 12 stay free when using existing bedding. an additional guest fee applies for extra adults beyond standard room occupancy.

payment and billing

what payment methods do you accept?

we accept all major credit cards, debit cards, and digital wallets. cash payments are accepted on-site at check-out.

will i be charged in advance for my stay?

a valid card is required to guarantee your booking, but full payment is only collected at check-in or check-out, depending on your rate type.
