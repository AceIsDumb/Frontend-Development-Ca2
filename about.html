about us

our story

founders photo — about-founders.jpg

the hotel opened in 1998 with eleven rooms, one shared kitchen, and a founding pair who had spent years working in other people's hotels and taking notes on what they would do differently.

what they wanted was simple and surprisingly rare: a place where the staff knew your name by the second morning, where nothing in the room needed a manual, and where the quiet was treated as a feature rather than an accident.

twenty-eight years later we have grown to sixty-four rooms across two buildings, and the founding pair still walk the floor most mornings. the notebook is still in use.

the second building came in 2014, an old post office next door that had been empty for six years. we kept the counter, the tiled floor, and the original clock above the door.

most of our staff have been here longer than a decade, which we think is the part guests actually notice, even if it is not the part they can point to.

what guides us

mission & values

mission

to give every guest a stay that asks nothing of them — a room that works the moment they walk in, and service that anticipates rather than interrupts.

vision

to become the hotel travellers return to by habit rather than by comparison, and to grow only at the pace at which we can keep that true.

values

care taken quietly, honesty in what we promise, restraint in what we add, and respect for the staff who make all of it possible.
