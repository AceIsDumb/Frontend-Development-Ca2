Our Story

The hotel opened in 1998 with eleven rooms, one shared kitchen, and a group of four who had spent years working in other people's hotels and taking notes on what they would do differently.

What they wanted was simple and surprisingly rare: a place where the staff knew your name by the second morning, where nothing in the room needed a manual, and where the quiet was treated as a feature.

Now, in the present, we have grown to sixty-four rooms across two buildings, and the group still walk the floor most mornings. the notebook is still in use.

The second building came in 2014, an old post office next door that had been empty for six years. we kept the counter, the tiled floor, and the original clock above the door.

Most of our staff have been here as long as the hotel's original opening, and we hope that it helps shapes the guests' experience, even if it is not the part they can point to.

What guides us · Notre philosophie

Mission & Valeurs

Mission

To give every guest a stay that asks nothing of them, a room that works the moment they walk in, and service that anticipates.

Vision

To become the hotel travellers return to by habit rather than by comparing the value by price, and to grow only at the pace at which we can keep that true.

Valeurs

Care taken quietly and professionally, honesty in what we promise, restraint in what we add, and respect for the staff who make all of it possible.
